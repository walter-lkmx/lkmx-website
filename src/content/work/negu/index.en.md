## Background

Negu provides premium bandwidth and connectivity solutions for superyachts, cruise ships, and private aircraft. The company specializes in delivering the fastest, most reliable connectivity with low-latency connections.

## Challenge

Negu required a clean, easy-to-maintain static website without a Content Management System (CMS) that accurately represented its brand and service offerings. 

## Solution

Logic Kernel designed and built a fast-loading website with a clean, modern aesthetic. The design emphasizes Negu's core offerings and commitment to high-quality connectivity.

![](/work/negu-figure-1.jpg)

## Impact

With its new digital presence, Negu was able to expand its business reach beyond reliance on person-to-person contact, facilitating broader market engagement.

![](/work/negu-figure-2.jpg)

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates Logic Kernel's consistent commitment to all its projects. Regardless of perceived simplicity, the same level of effort and dedication is applied to deliver excellent results.
