## Background

Mestyka specializes in domestic personnel management, distinguished by its strong client base and exceptional staff in Mexico.

## Challenge

The primary challenge involved streamlining and securing the process of hiring domestic personnel. 

## Solution

Logic Kernel developed a Progressive Web Application (PWA) for Mestyka. This application enables users to register, manage their profiles, search for personnel, oversee the hiring process, and access support services. For domestic staff, the PWA allows them to accept or decline job offers based on their needs, and manage their availability and rates.

![](/work/mestyka-figure-1.jpg)

## Impact

Transforming a traditionally referral-based hiring process, the PWA significantly accelerated personnel placement. It also empowered domestic staff by giving them the autonomy to choose offers that best fit their preferences and schedules.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates Logic Kernel's capability to convert an analog process into a fully digital and scalable experience. The solution showcases a commitment to enhancing client operations and user experience.
