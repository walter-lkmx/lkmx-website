## Challenge

NEGU, a company specializing in high-speed connectivity and streaming access from anywhere in the world —even on superyachts in the middle of the ocean—, needed an informational brochure that clearly communicated its benefits and competitive advantages. The challenge was to translate technical information into a simple and engaging format for audiences with limited technical knowledge, while highlighting the reach of NEGU’s network and its superiority over competitors. Additionally, the design had to be versatile enough to work both as a printed brochure and as slides for presentations.

## Solution

A clean and modern brochure design was created, combining clear graphics, illustrative maps, and visual comparisons to make the network’s coverage easy to understand. A well-defined information hierarchy was developed to enable quick reading, supported by visual elements that avoided overwhelming the reader with technical data. The layout was also designed to be flexible, allowing each section to be seamlessly adapted into slides for corporate presentations.

## Conclusion

The brochure allowed NEGU to communicate its value proposition in a clear, attractive, and professional way, providing a versatile tool for both print and presentation formats. With a design focused on visual clarity and strategic comparisons, NEGU now has a strong resource to showcase the reach and quality of its service in commercial and marketing contexts.
